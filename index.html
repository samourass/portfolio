About me

Defending systems from log to model

A cybersecurity engineer in training who enjoys building, monitoring and breaking things — safely.

I'm a final-year Cybersecurity Engineering student at TEK-UP University, completing an Engineering Degree in Computer Science with a specialization in cybersecurity (expected July 2027).

My hands-on work covers SOC & SIEM — deploying IBM QRadar during my internship at Tunisie Telecom and building Wazuh/Suricata monitoring with threat intelligence and SOAR — as well as DevSecOps pipelines with SAST, SCA and DAST, secure AWS and Kubernetes infrastructure, and malware analysis in isolated labs.
